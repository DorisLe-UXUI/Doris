Our story

Made by families who wanted better for their kids

Kingdomland Kids started on YouTube, where more than 159,000 families found Lucy, Leo and a growing library of Bible stories. Parents kept asking for one thing: a place to watch without ads, autoplay or algorithms. So we built it.

From a channel to a kingdom

The first Marvelous Light episodes were made for the kids in our own living rooms. They watched a lantern light up a world of talking animals and asked the same questions their parents had asked in Sunday school — and then asked to watch again.

YouTube took the stories further than we imagined. It also showed us the problem: the moment an episode ended, the next thing on screen wasn't ours. Kingdomland Kids is the answer — one app where every video, song and game has been made or chosen for our families, and where "next" is always safe.

What "made for our families" means

African American families have carried the faith for generations, and our kids deserve to see themselves in its stories. Every Kingdomland series is voiced by an African American cast. Black History Beats sets the courage of Jackie Robinson, Bessie Coleman and Dr. King to music, because those are Bible stories too — stories of faith under pressure.

What we believe

Three promises to every parent

Scripture first

Every series starts in the Bible and stays there. If an episode teaches a lesson, it's because the passage does.

Representation matters

Kids learn who belongs in God's story by who they see in it. Ours see themselves — on screen and in the voice cast.

Screen time that gives back

No ads, no autoplay, no algorithm. Memory verses, quiz games and discussion prompts turn watching into something you can talk about at dinner.
